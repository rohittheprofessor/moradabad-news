Privacy Policy for Moradabad News

Last updated: 5 June 2026

Moradabad News is a Hindi-first local news application for Moradabad, Rampur, Sambhal, Amroha, Bareilly, and nearby districts. This Privacy Policy explains how the app collects, uses, stores, and protects user information.

Information We Collect

If you sign in with Google, the app may collect your Firebase user ID, name, email address, and profile photo URL. The app also stores your saved bookmarks so that you can access bookmarked news articles later.

The app may receive a device messaging token from Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver breaking news alerts, daily news digests, and weather alerts.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to provide login, bookmark saving, notifications, language preferences, and news delivery features. We do not sell personal data.

News, Summaries, and Third-Party APIs

The app displays news collected from third-party news providers such as GNews or NewsAPI. Article summaries may be generated using OpenAI services when API quota is available. These services process article content for summarization; personal user data is not intentionally sent for news summarization.

Firebase Services

The app uses Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and related Firebase infrastructure. Firebase may process technical data needed to provide authentication, database, and notification services.

Data Sharing

We may share limited data with service providers used by the app, including Firebase, Google Sign-In, news API providers, and OpenAI, only as needed to operate the app. We do not sell user information to advertisers or data brokers.

Data Security

We use Firebase security rules and standard cloud security practices to protect stored data. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

Data Retention

Account profile data and bookmarks are retained while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account data by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Moradabad News is a general news app and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your Choices

You can choose not to sign in with Google. You can disable notifications from Android system settings. You can request deletion of your account data by contacting us.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact: rohitmtech1988@gmail.com
